## Getting There

> Full directions PDF: [Directions for visitors (Merck)](https://www.merckgroup.com/de/company/darmstadt-site/visitors-contractors/en/Directions-for-visitors.pdf)

**Visitor address (Google Maps):** Frankfurter Str. 250, 64293 Darmstadt

### By Car / Motorcycle
Follow signs to visitor parking **Building F133** on Frankfurter Straße.  
From motorways A5, A67, A661 → via B3.

### By Public Transport
Tram to stop **"Merck"** (lines 1, 6, 8).  
Regional trains: **Nordbahnhof** stop (RB75, RB80, RB82) is nearby.

### By Bicycle
Covered bicycle parking in front of visitor parking area F133 on Frankfurter Straße.

### By Plane
- Nearest airport(s): Frankfurt International Airport (FRA)
- Transfer options: 
    - Bus "AirLiner": Frankfurt Airport (Terminal 1) to Darmstadt Mainstation
    - Trains via Frankfurt Mainstation to Darmstadt Nord

### Visitor Registration
**Pre-registration is required**. After confirmed registration, you will get an info mail with more details including a link to complete your visitor check-in. 
>Further details can be found here: [Visitor Information (Merck, Darmstadt Site)](https://www.merckgroup.com/de/company/darmstadt-site/visitors-contractors/en/Welcome-DA-brochure-EN.pdf)
Bring your **ID card**. Register at **Visitor Reception, Building B31 – Innovation Center**.
